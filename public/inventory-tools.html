Dead stock / slow-moving report

Stock movement ledger

Read-only timeline of recorded stock movements: sales, restocked returns, POs, consignment, waste/batch loss and count adjustments. Manual stock edits, quick restocks and transfers are not included.

Shrinkage / variance report

Combines all real stock loss: count variance (shortages), waste/damage, damaged returns and batch loss, with a peso value (based on cost). Count overages are shown separately.

Smart reorder (based on sales speed)

Calculates "days until stockout" from average sales (the last 7 days are weighted more heavily), and the quantity to order so the stock lasts through the lead time + cover days. Quantities already on order (open POs) are deducted. If a supplier has 2 or more received POs, its actual average lead time is used instead of the default.

Near-expiry action list

More than just an alert: it checks whether the lot can still be sold before it expires (based on sales speed). If not, it suggests a markdown that never goes below cost. An expired lot can be disposed of directly into the Waste Log (deducted from stock).

Supplier cost history & price-change alerts

Records the cost of every received Purchase Order (enter the Cost (₱) when receiving the PO). Warns you when a supplier's cost goes up or when a cheaper supplier exists for the same product.
